# A Second Life for Our Glass

Oct 14, 2026

Dear [Neighborhood Name] neighbors,

When the county stopped collecting glass for recycling, most of our bottles and jars started going straight to the landfill. Glass can be recycled endlessly without losing quality, so that felt like a waste we didn't have to accept.

So we decided to fix it ourselves, right here on our own streets. We're writing to invite you to join a neighbor-run glass pickup service, and to help us keep it going.

## How it works

It's simple: you set your glass out, and we take it from there.

1. **Rinse and set it out.** Put your bottles and jars in a bin at the curb on pickup day, [every other Saturday morning].
2. **We collect.** Our crew picks it up on an efficient, planned route through the neighborhood.
3. **You get proof.** We snap a quick photo of your curb at every stop, so you'll know your glass was picked up. Photos show only the bin, never people.
4. **We recycle it.** Everything goes to the Arlington County Recycling Center, where it becomes new glass instead of landfill.

## Why it matters

**It keeps glass out of the landfill.** Every jar and bottle we collect gets a second life instead of sitting in the ground for centuries.

**It gives local teens their first real job.** The pickups are done by teenagers from our own neighborhood. Most of your subscription goes directly to them, and they learn what it takes to run a real service: showing up on schedule, planning routes, and looking after customers.

**It's neighbors helping neighbors.** This isn't a company. It's people you see on your street, doing something good together, right where we live.

## What it costs

The service is a flat **$8 per month**. That covers every pickup, whether you have a full bin or just one jar that week.

**Most of that money goes straight to our local teen helpers.** The rest covers fuel and recycling-center fees. Keeping the fee flat means the teens can count on steady pay, and the service stays dependable for everyone.

Going away? Just tell us and we'll **pause your account for the months you're gone**. You won't be billed for them. You'll also get a simple monthly statement showing your pickups and balance, so there are no surprises.
